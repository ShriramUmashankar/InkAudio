Top loop

Everything starts and ends at WAIT — the server idle, waiting for a PDF. Uploading one starts the generate lane (top, green highlights). The blue-labelled lane across the bottom is the revise path: feedback on a finished episode lands in EDITOR, gets critiqued, and only after approval are edits merged, re-spoken and re-stitched — then the whole thing funnels back to WAIT.

The demo always runs the full story: ingest, the optional questions path, the writer/critic loop (two rejections, then approval on the third attempt), TTS + stitching, and the revision lane. The dashed QUESTIONS/EVALUATOR nodes just show that their lane is optional.
